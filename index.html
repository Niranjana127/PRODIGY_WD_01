Academic Departments

Our departments are structured to provide strong fundamentals, hands-on experience, and exposure to emerging technologies.

Computer Science & Engineering

Focuses on programming, data structures, algorithms, artificial intelligence, machine learning, cloud computing, cybersecurity, and software engineering.

Key Areas: AI, ML, Data Science, Web & App Development

Information Technology

Emphasizes the application of computing technologies in real-world systems, including networking, databases, cloud services, and enterprise applications.

Key Areas: Web Technologies, Networks, Cloud Systems

Electronics & Communication Engineering

Deals with electronic circuits, communication systems, embedded systems, VLSI design, and signal processing technologies.

Key Areas: Embedded, VLSI, IoT, Communication

Electrical & Electronics Engineering

Covers power generation, transmission, electrical machines, control systems, and renewable energy technologies.

Key Areas: Power Systems, Control, Renewable Energy

Mechanical Engineering

Focuses on design, manufacturing, thermal engineering, robotics, and industrial automation.

Key Areas: Robotics, CAD/CAM, Manufacturing

Civil Engineering

Deals with planning, design, construction, and maintenance of infrastructure such as buildings, roads, bridges, and water systems.

Key Areas: Structures, Construction, Environmental Engg
